About Systel Group

Egypt's Trusted Leader in Telecom Solutions

Founded in 1983, Systel was established as the sole official sales and services distributor for Motorola Solutions in Egypt. Starting with two-way radio networks, we've grown to serve a wide range of government and commercial entities across the region with comprehensive telecom and broadband solutions.

Today, Systel maintains sales, software development, training, manufacturing, maintenance, and administrative facilities throughout Egypt and across the region.
